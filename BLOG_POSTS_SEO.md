<div class="blog-content">
    <p class="lead">Your clothes are an investment — in your style, your confidence, and your wallet. At <strong>SmartShop</strong>, we want every piece you buy to look amazing for years to come. That's why we've created this comprehensive care guide with expert tips from our fashion specialists.</p>

    <h2>Why Proper Care Matters</h2>
    <p>Proper clothing care isn't just about looking good — it's about:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Saving money:</strong> Clothes last longer, reducing replacement costs</li>
        <li><strong>Looking better:</strong> Well-maintained pieces always look more polished</li>
        <li><strong>Helping the planet:</strong> Extended garment life reduces waste</li>
        <li><strong>Preserving quality:</strong> SmartShop's premium pieces deserve premium care</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Washing Wisdom</h2>

    <h3>Read the Label (Always!)</h3>
    <p>Care labels are your first stop. Here's what common symbols mean:</p>
    <ul>
        <li>🛁 <strong>Washtub:</strong> Washing instructions</li>
        <li>🔺 <strong>Triangle:</strong> Bleaching guidelines</li>
        <li>⬜ <strong>Square:</strong> Drying instructions</li>
        <li>🔥 <strong>Iron:</strong> Ironing temperature</li>
        <li>⭕ <strong>Circle:</strong> Dry cleaning info</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Water Temperature Guide</h3>
    <table class="care-table">
        <tr>
            <th>Temperature</th>
            <th>Best For</th>
        </tr>
        <tr>
            <td><strong>Cold (30°C/86°F)</strong></td>
            <td>Darks, delicates, denim, anything that might shrink</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
            <td><strong>Warm (40°C/104°F)</strong></td>
            <td>Colors, synthetics, everyday wear</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
            <td><strong>Hot (60°C/140°F)</strong></td>
            <td>Whites, towels, bedding, heavily soiled items</td>
        </tr>
    </table>

    <h3>SmartShop Washing Tips</h3>
    <ul>
        <li>Turn clothes inside out to protect colors and prints</li>
        <li>Use mesh bags for delicates and items with straps</li>
        <li>Zip zippers and button buttons before washing</li>
        <li>Don't overload the machine — clothes need room to move</li>
        <li>Use gentle detergent for premium fabrics</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Drying Dos and Don'ts</h2>

    <h3>Air Drying (Recommended)</h3>
    <p>Air drying is gentler on fabrics and saves energy:</p>
    <ul>
        <li>Lay knits flat to prevent stretching</li>
        <li>Hang shirts on padded hangers</li>
        <li>Avoid direct sunlight for colored items</li>
        <li>Reshape while damp for best results</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Machine Drying</h3>
    <p>When using a dryer:</p>
    <ul>
        <li>Use low heat for most items</li>
        <li>Remove clothes while slightly damp to reduce wrinkles</li>
        <li>Clean the lint filter every time</li>
        <li>Add dryer balls to reduce drying time naturally</li>
        <li>Avoid over-drying — it damages fibers</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Fabric-Specific Care</h2>

    <h3>Cotton</h3>
    <p>Machine washable, but may shrink. Wash in cold water and air dry when possible.</p>

    <h3>Silk</h3>
    <p>Hand wash or dry clean only. Never wring — gently press out water and lay flat to dry.</p>

    <h3>Wool</h3>
    <p>Hand wash in cold water with wool-specific detergent. Lay flat to dry away from heat.</p>

    <h3>Polyester & Synthetics</h3>
    <p>Machine washable and quick-drying. Low heat to prevent melting or damage.</p>

    <h3>Denim</h3>
    <p>Wash inside out in cold water. Air dry to maintain shape and color. Wash less frequently.</p>

    <h3>Linen</h3>
    <p>Machine washable but wrinkles easily. Remove from dryer while damp and iron immediately.</p>

    <h2>Storage Solutions</h2>
    <p>How you store clothes matters just as much as how you wash them:</p>

    <ul>
        <li><strong>Use proper hangers:</strong> Padded for delicates, wooden for heavy items</li>
        <li><strong>Fold knits:</strong> Hanging stretches sweaters out of shape</li>
        <li><strong>Allow breathing room:</strong> Don't overcrowd your closet</li>
        <li><strong>Use garment bags:</strong> Protect special occasion pieces</li>
        <li><strong>Keep it cool and dry:</strong> Prevent mold and mildew</li>
        <li><strong>Rotate seasonally:</strong> Store off-season items properly</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Stain Removal Quick Reference</h2>
    <p>Act fast! The sooner you treat a stain, the better your chances:</p>

    <ul>
        <li><strong>Wine:</strong> Blot, don't rub. Apply salt or club soda immediately</li>
        <li><strong>Oil:</strong> Sprinkle with cornstarch, let sit, then wash</li>
        <li><strong>Blood:</strong> Cold water only (hot water sets it). Use hydrogen peroxide</li>
        <li><strong>Grass:</strong> Pre-treat with stain remover or vinegar solution</li>
        <li><strong>Coffee:</strong> Blot with cold water, then treat with enzyme cleaner</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>The SmartShop Quality Promise</h2>
    <p>When you shop at SmartShop, you're getting quality pieces designed to last. Follow these care tips, and your favorite SmartShop finds will stay beautiful for years.</p>
